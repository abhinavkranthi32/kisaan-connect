✅ 1. What is Completed & Working in Real Time
Fully Functional
⚙️ A. Central Real-Time Engine (server.js)
  • ✅
    SSE Event Stream Endpoint (GET /api/events)
    Native HTTP streaming (zero external framework dependency), headers configured with text/event-stream, no-cache, and cross-origin permissions.
  • ✅
    25-Second Heartbeat Ping
    Sends : ping\n\n periodically to prevent mobile telecom gateways and proxies from closing idle TCP sockets.
  • ✅
    Authenticated Client Mapping & Role Separation
    Maps connection tokens to genuine user IDs and roles (farmer, buyer, logistics, admin) for targeted event routing.
  • ✅
    Instant Snapshot on Handshake
    Immediately pushes live active harvest lots and available logistics trips upon connection establishment.
  • ✅
    Targeted Privacy Filtering (authFilter)
    Dispatches sensitive bid details strictly to the affected farmer, bidder, and admin, while emitting public price updates to the rest of the market.
🌾 B. End-to-End Procurement Lifecycle Synchronization
  • ✅
    Instant Lot Creation (Farmer → Buyer)
    When a farmer posts a harvest lot, NEW_LOT / listing.created fires; Buyer marketplace auto-renders the new lot without manual reload.
  • ✅
    Real-Time Bidding & Audio Alert (Buyer → Farmer)
    When a buyer bids, NEW_BID / bid.created triggers an audio chime, Telugu popup alert, and table update on the farmer's screen.
  • ✅
    Deal Acceptance & Escrow Lock (Farmer → Logistics)
    Accepting a bid locks buyer escrow, cancels competing bids, generates a pickup OTP, and pushes ORDER_CREATED directly to the Transporter board.
  • ✅
    Fleet Dispatch & Driver Assignment (Logistics → Farmer/Buyer)
    Transporter accepting trip emits TRIP_ASSIGNED & ORDER_UPDATED; Buyer and farmer see the driver's name and vehicle registration live.
  • ✅
    Pickup OTP Delivery & Instant Escrow Settlement
    Verifying OTP at destination emits ORDER_DELIVERED & ESCROW_RELEASED; Farmer wallet balance credits immediately with audit logging.
